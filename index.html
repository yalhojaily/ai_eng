Learn to build real products on top of language models, and learn what language itself is made of along the way. Every project here is a linguistics project, because the thing you are engineering with is a model of language.

How each week works

Two lessons. Each one mixes explanation with exercises you do right on the page, a short checkpoint quiz, and a lab you run on your own machine.

Reading duties. One assigned reading per week, mostly from Jurafsky & Martin, tied to that week's linguistics thread.

A project per phase. Labs feed the project, so by the end of a phase you have something shipped, with numbers to show for it. Budget 8 to 10 hours a week. Progress is saved in this browser.
